## Presentation and navigation

Pages use the public semantic theme tokens, wide responsive shell, readable legal measure, visible breadcrumbs, and an accessible mobile navigation menu. Header navigation is Rentals, How it works, About, and Contact. Footer links are grouped under Company and Legal. The pages are checked at 320, 375, 390, 430, 768, 1024, and 1440 pixels in light and dark modes.

About and Contact remain available regardless of optional operational feature states. Rental CTAs always keep the catalogue reachable; if online rental requests are unavailable, About states that clearly instead of presenting a broken submission promise.

## SEO

All four pages have unique titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, Open Graph metadata, Twitter metadata, and WebPage-family JSON-LD. About uses `AboutPage`; Contact uses `ContactPage` and an Organization projection containing only verified email, telephone, and locality. No `LocalBusiness`, street address, hours, founding date, price, inventory availability, rating, or review schema is invented.

The four canonical pages are included in the sitemap. They remain independent of private customer flows, Admin, and feature-control records.
